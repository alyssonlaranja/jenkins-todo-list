# Story Draft Validation Checklist

Comprehensive validation framework for ensuring user story quality, completeness, and readiness for development in AVANADE Method projects.

## 1. STORY FUNDAMENTALS VALIDATION
- [ ] Story follows proper user story format ("As a... I want... So that...")
- [ ] User role/persona is clearly identified and valid
- [ ] Business value and outcome are explicitly stated
- [ ] Story is written from user perspective, not system perspective
- [ ] Story describes WHAT and WHY, not HOW
- [ ] Story title is concise and descriptive

## 2. INVEST CRITERIA VALIDATION
- [ ] **Independent**: Story can be developed independently of other stories
- [ ] **Negotiable**: Story details can be discussed and refined
- [ ] **Valuable**: Story delivers clear business value to users
- [ ] **Estimable**: Story can be estimated by development team
- [ ] **Small**: Story can be completed within one sprint
- [ ] **Testable**: Story has clear testable acceptance criteria

## 3. ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA QUALITY
- [ ] Acceptance criteria are specific and measurable
- [ ] Criteria cover all functional aspects of the story
- [ ] Criteria include positive and negative scenarios
- [ ] Edge cases and boundary conditions are addressed
- [ ] Criteria are testable and verifiable
- [ ] Non-functional requirements are included where relevant

## 4. STORY COMPLETENESS VALIDATION
- [ ] All required fields are populated (ID, Title, Description, etc.)
- [ ] Story priority is assigned and justified
- [ ] Story size/points estimation is provided
- [ ] Dependencies on other stories are identified
- [ ] Assumptions and constraints are documented
- [ ] Definition of done is clear and achievable

## 5. BUSINESS CONTEXT VALIDATION
- [ ] Story aligns with product vision and strategy
- [ ] Business justification is clear and compelling
- [ ] User need is validated and research-backed
- [ ] Story supports defined business objectives
- [ ] ROI or business impact is articulated
- [ ] Story fits within product roadmap and priorities

## 6. TECHNICAL READINESS VALIDATION
- [ ] Technical approach is feasible and understood
- [ ] Required APIs and integrations are identified
- [ ] Data requirements and sources are specified
- [ ] Technical dependencies and risks are documented
- [ ] Architecture and design implications are considered
- [ ] Development effort estimation is realistic

## 7. UX/UI DESIGN ALIGNMENT
- [ ] User experience flow is defined and logical
- [ ] UI mockups or wireframes are provided (if needed)
- [ ] Interaction patterns are consistent with design system
- [ ] Accessibility requirements are considered
- [ ] Mobile/responsive design needs are addressed
- [ ] Usability and user testing requirements are defined

## 8. QUALITY ASSURANCE READINESS
- [ ] Story is testable with clear pass/fail criteria
- [ ] Test scenarios can be derived from acceptance criteria
- [ ] Test data requirements are identified
- [ ] Performance testing needs are considered
- [ ] Security testing requirements are defined
- [ ] Integration testing scenarios are planned

## 9. STORY DEPENDENCIES & RELATIONSHIPS
- [ ] Prerequisite stories are identified and planned
- [ ] Dependent stories are mapped and sequenced
- [ ] Integration points with other teams are defined
- [ ] External dependencies are documented and managed
- [ ] Risk dependencies are identified and mitigated
- [ ] Story relationships are visualized in story mapping

## 10. STAKEHOLDER ALIGNMENT
- [ ] Product owner has reviewed and approved story
- [ ] Key stakeholders have provided input and feedback
- [ ] Development team has reviewed for technical feasibility
- [ ] UX designer has validated user experience aspects
- [ ] QA team has confirmed testability requirements
- [ ] Business analyst has validated requirements completeness

## 11. EPIC & THEME ALIGNMENT
- [ ] Story aligns with parent epic objectives
- [ ] Story contributes to epic success criteria
- [ ] Story fits within epic timeline and scope
- [ ] Story is properly categorized within product themes
- [ ] Story supports overall product strategy and vision
- [ ] Story sequence within epic is logical and optimal

## 12. SPRINT READINESS VALIDATION
- [ ] Story meets "Definition of Ready" criteria
- [ ] Story can be completed within sprint timeframe
- [ ] Required resources and skills are available
- [ ] Story blocking issues are resolved
- [ ] Story has been refined and estimated by team
- [ ] Story is properly prioritized within sprint backlog

## 13. COMPLIANCE & GOVERNANCE
- [ ] Story meets organizational standards and guidelines
- [ ] Regulatory and compliance requirements are addressed
- [ ] Security and privacy requirements are included
- [ ] Documentation and audit trail requirements are met
- [ ] Change management implications are considered
- [ ] Risk and issue management processes are followed

## 14. STORY REFINEMENT QUALITY
- [ ] Story has been through proper refinement process
- [ ] Team questions and clarifications have been addressed
- [ ] Story details have been elaborated appropriately
- [ ] Estimation confidence level is acceptable
- [ ] Story scope is clearly defined and bounded
- [ ] Story is ready for sprint planning discussion

## 15. COMMUNICATION & DOCUMENTATION
- [ ] Story is documented in appropriate tool/system
- [ ] Story details are accessible to all team members
- [ ] Story updates and changes are tracked and communicated
- [ ] Story conversation and decisions are documented
- [ ] Story template and standards are followed consistently
- [ ] Story knowledge is shared with relevant stakeholders

## QUALITY GATES
- **Mandatory Gates** (Must Pass):
  - [ ] INVEST criteria validation (100% compliance)
  - [ ] Acceptance criteria completeness and quality
  - [ ] Technical feasibility confirmation
  - [ ] Product owner approval

- **High Priority Gates** (>90% compliance):
  - [ ] Business value articulation
  - [ ] Story size appropriateness
  - [ ] Dependency identification and management
  - [ ] UX/UI design alignment

## STORY APPROVAL WORKFLOW
- [ ] **Draft Review**: Initial story content and structure validation
- [ ] **Refinement Review**: Detailed story elaboration and estimation
- [ ] **Ready Review**: Final validation against Definition of Ready
- [ ] **Sprint Planning**: Team commitment and sprint inclusion
- [ ] **Final Approval**: Product owner and stakeholder sign-off

## CONTINUOUS IMPROVEMENT
- [ ] Story quality metrics tracked and analyzed
- [ ] Team feedback collected and incorporated
- [ ] Story template and process improvements identified
- [ ] Best practices documented and shared
- [ ] Lessons learned captured and applied to future stories
